Permanent Lighting

Permanent Lighting for Every Season

Permanent lighting gives your home year-round curb appeal with a low-profile track system mounted along your roofline. Change colors, brightness, and schedules from your phone for holidays, game days, or everyday accent lighting.

No more hanging and taking down lights every year. Hooks Permanent Lighting, an independently owned partner company of Hooks Property Services, installs permanent lighting for homes and businesses in Lancaster, PA with a clean, careful install you can enjoy for years.

Permanent Lighting Features

  • Low-profile roofline track
  • App-controlled colors and patterns
  • Holiday and everyday lighting
  • Residential and commercial installs

How It Works:

  1. Call or request a free quote
  2. We plan the layout for your roofline
  3. We install the low-profile track and lights
  4. You control colors and schedules from your phone
