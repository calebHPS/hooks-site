Deep Clean & Regular Upkeep

We start by creating a spotless foundation. On your first visit, built-up grime, mineral residue, and existing hard water stains are carefully removed by hand.

From there, regular cleanings on your plan stop dirt, pollen, and hard water spots from building back up, so your windows stay clear between visits.

  • Interior and exterior glass
  • Frames and sills wiped down
  • Hard water spot removal
  • Streak-free finish

Built for Pennsylvania Conditions

Pennsylvania’s rain, temperature changes, and mineral-heavy water can cause long-term damage to neglected glass. When hard water evaporates, minerals like calcium, magnesium, and iron are left behind, forming white, brown, or rust-colored stains.

Without Regular Cleaning

These deposits can become permanent—leading to costly repairs or glass replacement. Our maintenance process helps prevent buildup before it becomes a problem.

Don’t wait until damage becomes irreversible. Regular maintenance protects your investment and keeps your windows crystal clear year-round.
