About Us

Built on Hard Work, Integrity, and Ownership

Hooks Property Services is a locally owned and owner-operated window cleaning company founded by Reaves Hooks, a student-athlete at Lancaster Bible College originally from Pawleys Island, South Carolina. With over five years of hands-on experience, the business was built on Christian principles, discipline, and a commitment to doing honest work the right way.

We believe that work reflects character. Every job matters. Every customer matters. These beliefs guide how we show up, how we communicate, and how we deliver professional window cleaning services across Lancaster, PA and Myrtle Beach, SC.

Our Values

At Hooks Property Services, our work is guided by clear values that influence every decision we make. We believe honesty builds trust, hard work earns respect, and personal ownership leads to better results. These values define how we serve homeowners and businesses in Lancaster, PA and Myrtle Beach, SC.

  • Integrity in communication and transparent pricing
  • Personal responsibility for every job we take on
  • Strong work ethic with no shortcuts
  • Respect for every home, property, and customer

A Local, Owner-Led Company

When you contact Hooks Property Services, you are not reaching a call center or a rotating crew. You are working directly with the owner. This ensures clear communication, consistent quality, and service delivered with personal care and accountability.

Being owner-operated allows us to maintain high standards, build long-term relationships, and take pride in every job we complete. Our goal is simple—to provide reliable, professional window cleaning service that homeowners can trust.
